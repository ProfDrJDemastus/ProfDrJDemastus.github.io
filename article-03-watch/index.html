How this works

Every Monday a script on GitHub searches OpenAlex titles and abstracts for each search term, separately for every ISSN of each journal, with no start date and an end date of 31 December 2026. A record is kept when its title, abstract or OpenAlex keywords contain one of the terms, so stemmed near-misses are dropped. Records with a core term form the core stream. Records with only adjacent terms form the adjacent stream.

The core terms are strong sustainability, weak sustainability and sustainability strength. The adjacent terms are greenwashing and its variants, business-as-usual, business case for sustainability, integral sustainability, integrated sustainability, sustainability maturity and true sustainability. The Mentions sustainability filter applies the stricter rule that adjacent records must also mention sustainability.

OpenAlex differs from the Web of Science Topic field. It searches titles and abstracts but not author keywords or Keywords Plus, and some records have no abstract in OpenAlex, which the list marks. Use it as a cross-check and monitor, not a replacement. The search settings are in search.json and journals.json.
